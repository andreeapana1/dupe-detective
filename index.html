What this analysis cannot prove

  • Similar ingredients do not make the same product. Ingredient lists show no amounts, so texture, shine and feel can differ. I bought Essence Extreme Shine as a TikTok dupe for Dior Addict Lip Maximizer, and it was less shiny and stickier.
  • The shop's data has gaps. My scraper read fields correctly, but Matas itself misses ingredients, sizes in ml and whole ingredient lists, and some lists may be out of date.
  • The real margins are unknown. Only Matas's group margin is public, and the result depends on how different the two products' margins are.
  • We don't know if customers will switch, or how many. The numbers show what one switch would cost.
  • No product is said to copy another.

How it was made

  1. 1

    Collect

    Lip gloss pages from Matas.dk, within robots.txt, with an honest scraper waiting 3 seconds between requests. Checked against the brands' own websites.

  2. 2

    Clean

    products → lip glosses, one shade per product. "May contain" ingredients and colours don't count.

  3. 3

    Match

    Similarity = share of ingredients two glosses have in common. Compared with my own judgements and ChatGPT's (we agreed on pairs).

  4. 4

    Margin

    Prices without 25% VAT × Matas's group gross margin from the 2024/25 annual report = gross profit per tube.
